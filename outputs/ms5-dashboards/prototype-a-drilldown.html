If I commit to a genre, what price and features do its successful games share?

Prototype A. Pick a genre in the bubbles, then a price band on the shelves. Each click narrows the next view. Data: Steam Games Dataset (Kaggle), 99,754 games with a non-empty owner estimate, top 10 genres.

1 · Genre reach (sketch RC5)

Which genres reach the most owners? Click a bubble to focus the views below on that genre.

2 · Price shelves (sketch JL3)

Within all genres, which price bands hold the largest share of successful games? Click a shelf to list its traits.

3 · Traits of the successful games (sketch JL5)

Each trait tile shows the share of successful games in the current selection that meet the condition, and below it the same share among successful games across all genres and prices. Filled box = meets condition. Review share needs ≥50 reviews.
